# Privacy Overview — J.A.R.V.I.S. Mark 54

**Effective Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Author:** Divyanshu Verma

J.A.R.V.I.S. Mark 54 is committed to transparency regarding how data is captured, processed, and transmitted.

---

## 🔒 Core Privacy Principles

1. **No Proprietary Telemetry**: J.A.R.V.I.S. Mark 54 does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or developer telemetry servers.
2. **Direct-to-Provider Communication**: All network traffic generated by AI features flows directly between your local Mac and official Google Gemini API servers over encrypted HTTPS/WSS connections.
3. **Local Credential Storage**: Your Google Gemini API key is stored locally in your user profile at `~/.config/jarvis/api_keys.json`. It is never transmitted to the project author or any secondary server.
4. **Explicit System Permissions**: Camera, microphone, screen recording, and automation actions are gated behind macOS system permissions (TCC).

---

## 📡 Sensor Data Usage

### Microphone
- **Usage**: Used to stream voice queries to Google Gemini Live API.
- **Processing**: Processed in real-time memory and streamed to Google. Audio is not recorded to local disk files by J.A.R.V.I.S.

### Camera
- **Usage**: Used for 24/7 Security Sentry and interactive visual inspection.
- **Processing**: Security sentry motion detection runs locally on your machine. When you explicitly ask vision questions, individual frames are encoded and submitted to Gemini.

### Screen Recording
- **Usage**: Used to capture snapshots of your desktop display when requested by voice or text.
- **Processing**: Frames are held in volatile RAM buffers and sent directly to Gemini as multimodal context.

### Accessibility & Automation
- **Usage**: Executes user-authorized desktop automations via AppleScript.
- **Processing**: Local execution only.

## ☁️ Third-Party AI Services

J.A.R.V.I.S. utilizes **Google Gemini** for inference:
- Text, audio, and visual inputs are processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [Google AI Privacy Policy](https://ai.google.dev/terms).
- If you use a standard Google AI Studio free tier key, Google may log prompts and responses for service improvement according to their developer terms. Users desiring zero-retention should review Google Cloud Vertex AI enterprise options.

## 🛡️ User Responsibilities

- **Protect Your Credentials**: Keep your API key file (`~/.config/jarvis/api_keys.json`) secure. We recommend restricting file permissions:
  ```bash
  chmod 600 ~/.config/jarvis/api_keys.json
  ```
- **Permission Auditing**: Periodically review granted permissions under **System Settings > Privacy & Security**.
